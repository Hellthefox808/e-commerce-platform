# 02 — Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Executive Intent
LuxeCommerce aims to maximize order conversion rates through frictionless checkout UX, transparent pricing, and instant payment confirmation, while enforcing fraud mitigation and security standards.

## Business Rules & Metrics (KPIs)
- **Cart Abandonment Reduction**: Provide quick guest checkout and saved customer payment/address profiles.
- **Conversion Rate Target**: 3.5% target store conversion rate supported by sub-second page load times.
- **Multi-Currency & Multi-Gateway Support**: Support USD ($) and INR (₹) with Stripe (Credit Card, Apple Pay) and Razorpay (UPI, Netbanking) integrations.
- **Promo Engine**: Dynamic discount codes supporting percentage off, fixed amount off, and minimum order threshold validation.
- **Audit & Compliance**: PCI-DSS compliant payment integration (hosted fields / tokenization), SOC 2 audit trail readiness.
